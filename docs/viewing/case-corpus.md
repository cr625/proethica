# Case Corpus and Sources

ProEthica is a research and teaching tool for studying published professional ethics opinions. The case collection consists of opinions of the Board of Ethical Review (BER) of the National Society of Professional Engineers (NSPE). ProEthica represents each opinion for inspection and comparison, and it labels the findings of the Board separately from its own analytical views.

## The Board and the Code

The NSPE Board of Ethical Review considers ethical cases submitted by NSPE members, other engineers, public officials and members of the public. It reviews each case in the context of the NSPE Code of Ethics for Engineers and its earlier opinions, and publishes its opinions under numbered case identifiers such as Case 24-1. NSPE publishes the facts of each case with the parties identified by role (Engineer A, Client X).

## Source Material

Each case is taken from its published opinion page on nspe.org. The stored case text keeps the sections of the opinion: Facts, Questions, NSPE Code References, Discussion, Conclusions and, where present, a Dissenting Opinion. The case number, the date given for the opinion on its NSPE page and the NSPE subject tags are stored with the case. [Browsing Cases](browsing-cases.md) describes the case page.

The collection on proethica.org contains 119 opinions issued between 1958 and 2024. Opinions that NSPE publishes later, such as Case 25-1, are added in subsequent releases. NSPE provides opinions issued before 1980 for historical reference, since the NSPE Code of Ethics and the applicable requirements have changed since; the year shown on each case page identifies them.

## Attribution and Reuse Conditions

NSPE permits reprinting a BER opinion without further permission, provided that the NSPE note is included before or after the text of the case and that appropriate attribution is given to the NSPE Board of Ethical Review. ProEthica provides both.

- **Attribution.** Each case page carries the line "Source: National Society of Professional Engineers, Board of Ethical Review, Case NN-N" directly after the opinion text, linked to the opinion page on nspe.org.
- **NSPE note.** The footer of the main application pages carries the NSPE note while the Engineering domain is selected. Its educational-use and reprint paragraph is always visible; the complete note opens from **Full NSPE note** and prints in full with the page.

The note reproduced is the current NSPE wording, as printed in recent opinions such as Cases 24-1 and 25-1.
