Three points where the tests stop short of the observed failure:

1. **The facet fold sees one response at a time.** `role_checks.fold_duplicate_relational_facets` (line 93) folds a relational facet into an earlier facet of the same actor *in the same list*, and `UnifiedDualExtractor._apply_component_checks` (extractor.py line 282) hands it the individuals of one pass. The test folds two facets from one list, a shape the case 8 record did not have. Reported below (item 5).
2. **The depended-on protection cannot fire in production.** `filter_individuals(..., depended_on=referenced_labels(_dicts))` (extractor.py line 328) collects the reference fields (`source`, `required_for_obligations`, `relies_on`, `depends_on`, `resource`) of the same resources batch. `ResourceIndividual` has none of these fields, and the entities that rested on the dropped resource in case 8 (the `source` of an obligation, the `required_for_obligations` of a capability) are extracted in step 2, after the step 1 resources filter has run. The test passes the set by hand. The Tier-1 reliance keep (`used_by`) is what covers O-19. Reported below (item 6).
3. **One test pins a choice, not an observation.** `test_self_instance_with_reliance_is_still_judged_not_kept_outright` fails on the base because the base dropped such a row deterministically and the branch sends it to the judge. The case 8 resource was not a self-instance (it reached the judge on the base too), so this test documents a deliberate widening rather than a case 8 failure. Reported below (item 9).

Two fixture-versus-producer checks passed. ## Question 2. Behaviour the rules do not name

### Fixed on the review branch

1. **A string-valued `generates_obligations` dropped the whole role individual.** `app/services/extraction/schemas.py`, `RoleIndividual.generates_obligations` (line 393). The structured-output grammar enforces the array, but the free-form fallback (`_GRAMMAR_TOO_LARGE`, or a provider without structured output) can return the one-duty case as a bare string or a null; `List[str]` rejects both, the full-schema parse fails and `_parse_items` skips the individual (parsing.py line 198). A role the model extracted was lost because of the new routing field. Fix: the before-validator folds a string into one entry and a null (or an empty string) into no entry, the fold `PrincipleIndividual` already applies to `balancing_with`. Tests: `test_bare_string_generates_obligations_is_one_entry`, `test_full_result_keeps_a_role_whose_generates_obligations_is_a_string`.

2. **The actor cue of the segmenter leaked a precedent recap that shares the present letter.** `app/services/extraction/discussion_segmenter.py`, `_split_recap_tail` (line 127) and the new `_usable_actor_cues` (line 109). The branch disabled a letter cue only when the opening sentence of the recap named that letter. A recap whose opening sentence names no engineer ("In BER Case 82-5, the Board reviewed a dispute over a building design.") and whose second sentence names its own Engineer A was split at that sentence whenever the engineer of the present case is also A, the usual letter across the corpus, and the rest of the recap reached every extractor as present-case text; a probe on the integration tip reproduced it. This is the contamination the segmenter exists to remove. Fix: the actor cue applies only when the opening sentence names an engineer foreign to the present case (the actor of the precedent is then identifiable), and the opening-sentence guard stays. The fixture of the branch keeps its splits (its recap heads name Engineer A and Engineer B against a present Engineer L). Trade-off: a recap whose opening sentence names no engineer loses the letter cue and its present-case tail depends on the phrase cues, as the case-60 shape already did. Tests: `test_shared_letter_named_after_the_opening_sentence_stays_recap`, `test_continuation_paragraph_of_a_precedent_keeps_its_sentences`, `test_actor_cue_still_splits_after_a_foreign_engineer_in_the_opening_sentence`.

3. **Snapped quotes were recorded as re-grounded.** `app/services/extraction/verification_gate.py` (`GateResult.snapped_only`, line 68; `gate_quote_marker`, line 275) and `app/tasks/pipeline_tasks.py` (line 1395). A row whose quotes were all verbatim and only changed spelling entered `corrected_quotes`, so the commit task appended "quotes re-grounded at gate" to its review_notes, the marker that says a paraphrase was replaced by a model-found span. Fix: the gate collects the snapped-only ids, reports them as `snapped_ids`, and `gate_quote_marker` gives the commit task "quotes snapped to the case span at gate" for them; the attached and re-grounded markers are unchanged. Tests: `test_gate_reports_snapped_only_rows`, `test_gate_quote_marker_distinguishes_the_three_cases`.

4. **The merge put the linked-row skip in front of the snap.** `app/services/extraction/verification_gate.py`, step 0b of `verify_case_entities` and `_linked_verbatim` (line 238, from commit-definitions) beside the snap of this branch. `_linked_verbatim` uses the token test, which accepts the D-06 spelling ("Engineer L" newline "'s scope of work"), so a row the matcher linked at 0.90 or above with that quote was left unjudged and never snapped; the committed literal kept the wrong characters for exactly the rows the skip was meant to spare a model call. Fix: `_snap_skipped_quotes` (line 254) rewrites the quotes of a skipped row to the span the case text spells, records the row as snapped-only and counts the change in the report; the skip itself (no model call) stands. Test: `test_linked_row_skipped_by_the_gate_is_still_snapped`.

### Reported, not changed

5. **The facet fold does not reach across passes.** `app/services/extraction/role_checks.py`, `fold_duplicate_relational_facets` (line 93). Scenario: the D-12 record itself, a relational facet minted in the discussion pass toward a counterparty the facts-pass facet (a stored row) already links. Proposed: hand the fold the prior-section role rows (`load_prior_section_rows` exists) as read-only hosts and drop a duplicate relational facet whose counterparties a stored facet already links, recording the drop in the role_checks pass report; or run the same check at reconcile, where both passes are rows and the standing can be carried onto the host row.

6. **The depended-on protection is inert.** `app/services/extraction/unified_dual_extractor/extractor.py`, `_filter_types_as_individuals` (line 328). The set it passes is always empty for resources (no reference field on `ResourceIndividual`, and the dependents are extracted later). Proposed: remove the argument from this call, and protect the resource where its dependents exist, at the storage-time dedup or at the commit gate, where the `source` of an obligation or the `required_for_obligations` of a capability names it.

7. **The Board-normative pattern is wider than normative sentences.** `app/services/temporal_dynamics/utils/happening_checks.py`, `BOARD_NORMATIVE_RE` (line 26). Bare `cannot`, `could not`, `should (have)` and `has an obligation to` match narrated sentences from the discussion: "Engineer L could not quantify the risk until the studies were complete" and "Engineer L has an obligation to notify the client, and did so after the studies" both route an action whose only references they are to modality prescribed (probed on the integration tip). Proposed: require the ethics adverb or deontic context the Board uses after the bare modal (`cannot` or `could not` followed within two words by `ethically` or `properly`), and keep the explicit forms (`must not`, `would not be ethical`, `is unethical`, `is obligated to`).

8. **The stray-tail strip removes three-letter file extensions.** `app/services/extraction/unified_dual_extractor/parsing.py`, `_STRAY_TAIL_RE` (line 42). A narrative literal ending in "stormwater.pdf" or "design.dwg" becomes "stormwater." and "design." (probed). The observed fragment ("insistence.on") had two letters. Proposed: allow one or two letters, or add the common document extensions to `_TLD_TAILS`.

9. **A self-instance with a reliance field now goes to the judge.** `app/services/extraction/individual_type_filter.py`, `_triage` (line 195). On the base a self-instance with no marker dropped deterministically; on the branch a populated `used_by` or `cited_by` makes it ambiguous, so the Tier-2 judge decides and, without a streaming provider, it is kept. The test documents the choice. Noted, since the rule names a Tier-1 keep for non-self-instances only.

10. **The closed state list reads every stored state row.** `app/tasks/pipeline_tasks.py`, `_case_state_labels` (line 152). The list takes every state individual of the case, every session and deselected rows included, so a label from an older run or a gate-dropped row stays admissible for initiates and terminates. Proposed: restrict to `is_selected` rows of the active extraction sessions. The Step 3 entry outside the Celery task passes no `state_labels`, so the check is off there by design.

11. **The prior-individuals block lists rows whatever their selection.** `app/services/extraction/unified_dual_extractor/prompt_building.py`, `load_prior_section_rows` (line 29). As the pre-existing class block, it carries no `is_selected` filter; between the facts and discussion passes the gate has not run, so the effect is small. Noted.

12. **Allen relations with paraphrased evidence are dropped outright.** `app/services/temporal_dynamics/extractors/temporal_marker_extractor.py`, `_ground_allen_evidence` (line 209). The prompt asks for a quote, so the rule stands; a model that paraphrases every evidence field loses every relation, and the drop is logged at INFO. Proposed: WARNING when the drops exceed half of the relations.

13. **The actor cue keeps one leak.** `discussion_segmenter._usable_actor_cues` (line 109, after fix 2). A recap whose opening sentence names a foreign engineer and whose later sentence names the present letter for the second engineer of the precedent still splits at that sentence. A letter-based cue cannot separate the two; a corpus count of recaps with two engineer letters would show whether it matters.

14. **The professional typing check depends on the directive reaching the model.** `role_checks.reject_unsupported_professional` (line 75) rejects role_kind professional whenever `generates_obligations` is empty. The code-side `_role_directives_block` asks for the field through `{{ role_directives }}`, which the live roles template carries since the base seed, and the structured schema shows the field; when a model still names no duty for a plainly professional role, role_kind goes unstated and the commit-side backstop (`category_resolution._extract_role_kind`) stays silent, with the occupational class chain typing the role. Noted.

15. **Grammar size.** `to_structured_output_schema` of the roles result grows from 10304 to 10593 characters (282 to 289 nodes) with `generates_obligations`, the actions result from 8010 to 8181 (228 to 238) with `modality`. The July probe found all nine schemas under the ceiling and `_GRAMMAR_TOO_LARGE` (llm_calls.py line 31) remains the runtime guard; the ceiling cannot be probed here. The fallback it selects is the free-form path on which fix 1 applied.

16. **The snap widens the quote rewrite.** `verification_gate.apply_corrected_quotes` collapses `source_texts` to the first span for every section and sets `source_text` to the first quote; the branch extends this rewrite from re-grounded rows to every row with a spelling difference. The shape is pre-existing and the rule asks for the characters of the case text in the committed literal. Noted.

17. **`modality` has no ontology declaration.** `rdf_converter.convert_action_to_rdf` emits `proeth:modality`; `proethica-intermediate.ttl` and `proethica-core.ttl` declare no such property (the modality obligation subclasses were retired in June). The declaration belongs with the OntServe review (brief 3).
